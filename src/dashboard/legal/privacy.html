Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy explains what the Rumbleverse Revival Discord bot (“the bot”) and its web dashboard at %BASE_URL% (“the dashboard”) store, why, and how you can have it removed. They are run by the Rumbleverse Revival team (“we”, “us”). The short version: we keep only what the bot’s features need to work, we don’t sell or share it, and we don’t use ads, trackers or analytics.

1. What we store

Discord identifies people, servers, channels and messages by numeric IDs. Most of what we store is those IDs, plus the things below, and only when the feature that needs them is used.

Server settings

The settings a server’s staff choose with /settings and the other setup commands or on the dashboard: channels, roles, automod rules, welcome messages, tags, auto-responses, role panels, followed feeds, sticky and scheduled messages, and similar. These belong to the server, not to individual members.

Moderation

  • Cases (warnings, timeouts, kicks, bans, notes): the member’s ID and username at the time, the moderator’s ID and username, the reason, any duration and the time.
  • Ban appeals sent from the dashboard: your answers, the ban reason, and the staff decision.
  • Saved roles: the roles taken away by a quarantine, or the roles you had when you left a server that uses role persistence, so they can be given back. Temporary roles and when they end.
  • Join and leave times for each member, shown in their history to staff.

Tickets

For each support ticket: who opened it, its subject and category, who closed it and why, and a transcript of the messages in the ticket, saved when it is closed.

Community features

  • Leveling (if a server turns it on): your XP, message count and username, and XP per day for the weekly and monthly boards.
  • Birthdays: month and day only, never the year.
  • Reminders: the text you asked to be reminded of, and when.
  • AFK: your away message and since when.
  • Highlights: the words you asked to be notified about.
  • Giveaways: who entered and who won.
  • Other features keep only IDs: starboard posts, join-to-create voice channels and their owners, and messages posted from the dashboard (with a short summary and who posted them).

The dashboard

  • When you log in with Discord we receive your user ID, username, avatar and the list of servers you are in (with your permissions there), which we use to show the servers you can manage. We keep your Discord access token, encrypted, for the length of your login. We never see your Discord password or email address.
  • Changes made on the dashboard are recorded with who made them, so each server’s staff can see its activity history.

Bot-wide totals

How many servers the bot is in, how many members those servers have in total, and how many commands have been run. These are totals only and are shown on the public home page.

Error logs

When something goes wrong, the bot writes an error log on our server. These lines can include server, channel or user IDs. They are used only to fix problems.

2. Messages

The bot reads messages in the channels it can see so that its features can work: automod, leveling, auto-responses, highlights, AFK replies, the starboard and the server log. It does this as messages arrive and does not save message content, except in these cases:

  • Ticket transcripts, as above.
  • Text you give the bot yourself: reminders, AFK messages, appeal answers, case reasons.
  • If a server turns on its server log, the bot posts edited and deleted messages to that server’s own log channel in Discord. Those posts are kept by Discord, in that server, under the control of its staff.

3. How we use it

Only to run the bot and the dashboard: carrying out commands, applying a server’s settings, showing staff their server’s records, and fixing problems. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, build profiles, or use it to train AI models.

4. Who can see it

  • The server’s staff. Records from a server (cases, appeals, tickets and transcripts, member history, dashboard activity) are shown to that server’s staff on Discord and the dashboard. Leaderboards, birthdays and giveaways are shown to the server’s members, as those features are meant to.
  • Discord, which carries everything the bot does. Discord’s own Privacy Policy applies to your use of Discord.
  • Our hosting provider, which runs the server the bot and its database are on.
  • Anyone else only if the law requires it.

Followed feeds (YouTube, Reddit, RSS) are fetched by our server from public addresses. No member information is sent to those sites.

5. Cookies and browser storage

  • rv_session: keeps you logged in to the dashboard, for up to 7 days.
  • rv_oauth_state: protects the Discord login step, for 10 minutes.
  • Your browser’s local storage remembers the dashboard language and look you pick. It never leaves your browser.

There are no advertising, analytics or third-party cookies.

If the bot is removed from a server, that server’s data stays stored so nothing is lost if it is added back. The server’s owner can ask us to delete it.

7. Your choices

  • Remove your own data with /birthday remove, /highlight clear and /remind cancel. Sending a message ends your AFK status.
  • Log out of the dashboard at any time; this deletes your login.
  • Server staff can delete cases, reset levels and clear settings for their server.
  • You can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, or to delete it, using the contact below. We will do this unless a record has to be kept for a server’s safety (for example the case behind a ban), in which case we will tell you why.
  • If you leave a server or stop using the bot, it no longer collects anything new about you there.

8. Security

The dashboard only works over HTTPS. Login sessions are stored hashed and Discord access tokens are encrypted, so a copy of the database can’t be used to log in. The dashboard checks your Discord permissions before showing or changing anything for a server. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable care.

9. Children

The bot is for Discord users, who must be at least 13 (or older where local law requires). We don’t knowingly collect information about anyone younger. If you think we have, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

If what the bot stores changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Continuing to use the bot after a change means you accept the updated policy.
